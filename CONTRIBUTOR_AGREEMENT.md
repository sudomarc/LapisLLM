# LapisLLM Contributor Agreement

**Project:** LapisLLM  
**Repository:** `sudomarc/LapisLLM`  
**Project owner:** GitHub account `sudomarc`

## Purpose

LapisLLM is an open-source project, but the project owner intends to keep
central ownership and control of the official upstream project, releases,
branding, and accepted contributions.

By submitting a contribution to this repository, you agree to the terms
below. A contribution includes source code, documentation, tests,
configuration, data-processing code, or other copyrightable material
submitted through a pull request, commit, issue, discussion, patch, or other
project-maintained channel for inclusion in LapisLLM.

## 1. Original work

You represent that:

1. you are legally entitled to submit the contribution;
2. the contribution is your original work, or you have obtained sufficient
   rights and permissions to submit it under this agreement;
3. to your knowledge, the contribution does not knowingly violate another
   person's or entity's intellectual-property rights; and
4. you will identify material known to be third-party content and provide
   applicable attribution and license information.

## 2. Copyright assignment for accepted contributions

To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, you assign to the project
owner all right, title, and interest in the copyright and other copyrightable
rights you own in a contribution that is accepted and incorporated into the
official LapisLLM project.

Where an assignment is not legally effective or is not permitted under the
applicable law, you instead grant the project owner a perpetual, worldwide,
irrevocable, transferable, sublicensable, royalty-free, non-exclusive license
to reproduce, modify, adapt, publish, distribute, publicly display, publicly
perform, sublicense, and otherwise use the contribution for LapisLLM and any
successor, fork maintained by the project owner, or other official project
purpose.

This grant includes the right for the project owner to relicense the accepted
contribution, alone or as part of the project, under another open-source or
proprietary license in the future.

## 3. No obligation to accept

Submitting a contribution does not require the project owner to merge,
publish, maintain, or otherwise use it. The project owner retains final
discretion over the contents of the official upstream repository.

## 4. Moral rights and similar rights

To the extent permitted by law, you waive and agree not to assert moral rights
or similar rights in the contribution against the project owner or official
LapisLLM distributions. Where a waiver is not permitted, you agree not to
exercise those rights in a way that prevents or materially interferes with the
permissions granted by this agreement.

## 5. Attribution

The project may preserve contributor names, commit history, and attribution
where technically and legally appropriate. Attribution does not create joint
ownership of the official project or grant control over the LapisLLM brand.

## 6. Third-party material

Do not submit code, datasets, model weights, documentation, or other material
whose license is incompatible with the project without first identifying the
license and obtaining approval from the project owner.

## 7. Agreement through pull requests

For contributions requiring this agreement, the pull request must contain an
explicit confirmation that the contributor has read and accepted this file.
The project owner may require a separately signed agreement where local law,
commercial use, or a material contribution makes that appropriate.

Suggested pull-request confirmation:

> I have read and agree to `CONTRIBUTOR_AGREEMENT.md`, and I have the legal
> right to submit this contribution under its terms.

## 8. No employment or partnership

This agreement does not create employment, agency, partnership, joint
ownership of the project, or authority to act for the project owner.

## 9. Governing law

This document is a project contribution template, not jurisdiction-specific
legal advice. The parties should replace this section with a jurisdiction and
formal execution process appropriate to the project owner's legal situation
before relying on the assignment provisions as a formal copyright-assignment
contract.

## 10. Questions

For contribution-process questions, open a GitHub Discussion or issue. For
legal questions concerning ownership or third-party rights, do not post
private legal information publicly.
