# Roadmap

Lapis 0.2.0 separates implemented foundations from active hardening and future scale work.

Implemented or actively maintained today: configuration/validation, logging and developer CLI, ByteLevel BPE tokenization, the decoder-only Transformer with RMSNorm/RoPE/GQA/SwiGLU, training with monitoring and checkpoints, evaluation/generation, the LapisRuntime inference surface, and the local OpenAI-style API.

Future work includes stronger resume semantics, exact mid-epoch replay, more scalable data handling, distributed training and multi-GPU support, higher-performance inference, expanded evaluation, post-training, and larger model releases. No public deadlines are implied.
