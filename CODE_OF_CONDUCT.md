# Code of Conduct

LapisLLM is an open-source engineering and research project. Participation is expected to be professional, respectful and technically constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Focus criticism on code, data, research claims and decisions, not people.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels and technical opinions.
- Make issues and reviews specific, reproducible and actionable.
- Protect private information, credentials, security reports and personal data.
- Acknowledge uncertainty and correct errors when evidence changes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, targeted insults, doxxing, publication of private information, credential theft, deliberate disruption and abusive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement and reporting

The project maintainer may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions or restrict participation to protect the project and community.

For security vulnerabilities, follow SECURITY.md and do not use a public issue. For conduct concerns, contact the maintainer through a private channel listed on the repository owner's GitHub profile.
