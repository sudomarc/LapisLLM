# LapisLLM Code of Conduct

LapisLLM is an open-source engineering project. Participation is expected to
be professional, technical, and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and specific in issues, discussions, reviews, and pull requests.
- Respect different levels of experience and technical opinions.
- Focus criticism on code, architecture, documentation, or decisions rather
  than people.
- Protect private information, credentials, security reports, and personal data.
- Follow the project's security, licensing, and contribution rules.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, targeted insults, doxxing, malicious
publication of private information, credential theft, deliberate disruption,
and other abusive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

The project owner and authorized maintainers may edit, close, lock, or remove
content; restrict participation; or reject contributions when necessary to
protect the project and its community.

Serious or repeated violations may result in permanent loss of contribution
or discussion privileges.

## Reporting

For security vulnerabilities, do not use public issues. Follow `SECURITY.md`.
For community conduct concerns, contact the project owner through the private
contact method available on the GitHub profile or repository.

This code of conduct is intentionally concise and does not replace GitHub's
Terms of Service or applicable law.
